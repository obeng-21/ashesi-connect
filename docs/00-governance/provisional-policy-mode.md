# Provisional policy-based graduation assessment

This mode uses Ashesi's published policies and the supplied 2028 curriculum materials as working inputs. No written Registry signoff is required for a **provisional assessment**. The output must always state `officialCertification: false`.

Official policies and source links:
- https://ashesi.edu.gh/student-life/student-handbook/
- https://ashesi.edu.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Ashesi-Faculty-Policy-Handbook-2023-2024.pdf

Requirements include major/prerequisite D+; other undergraduate core/electives D; cumulative GPA >=2.0; repeated-course credits counted once; service learning; engineering internship where applicable; Writing Across the Curriculum Lab for applicable cohorts; and financial clearance.

The university may amend its rules. Curriculum entries and historical cohorts must be versioned, with source URL, publication/effective date and change history. Do not silently alter previously completed audits. A mismatch between the workbook and official published material must be flagged, not guessed.

**Not yet implemented**: full programme-specific 2028 workbook normalization, cohort selection, grade-point calculations from transcripts, authorized retake adjudication, IAP, alternate math tracks, and secure Registry workflows. Until complete and tested, `eligibilityDetermined` must remain false for unverified programme datasets. Do not generate official graduation certificates or falsely attribute Registry approval.

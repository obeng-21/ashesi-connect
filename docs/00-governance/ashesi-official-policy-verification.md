## Verified university-wide undergraduate policies
- Major and prerequisite courses: D+ minimum.
- Other core and elective courses: D minimum.
- Graduation requires cumulative GPA >= 2.0 and exit from probation.
- Repeat-course credits count once; third attempt requires approval; unauthorized repeats cannot be treated as valid.
- Engineering internships are graduation requirements.
- Academic Registry is the custodian of student academic records and authoritative degree requirement approvals.

## Implementation policy
A public website is evidence for general rules, not an official approval of the 2028 spreadsheet. The certification gate must remain fail-closed until a designated Registry officer approves a versioned curriculum and student transcript. Do not fabricate approval IDs or automatically mark the workbook verified. Audit eligibility estimates are not official graduation certificates.
